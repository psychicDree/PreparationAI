## 🧠 Color Psychology Research

### Blue Psychology
- **Trust & Reliability**: Blue is associated with stability, trust, and professionalism
- **Technology**: Widely used in tech companies (Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn)
- **Calming Effect**: Reduces anxiety and promotes focus
- **Professional**: Conveys competence and expertise

### Purple Psychology
- **Creativity & Innovation**: Associated with imagination and originality
- **Luxury & Premium**: Often used for high-end products
- **Wisdom & Intelligence**: Represents knowledge and sophistication
- **AI/Tech**: Popular in AI and cutting-edge technology brands

### Current Combination Benefits
- **Blue-Purple Gradient**: Combines trust (blue) with innovation (purple)
- **Perfect for AI**: Represents both reliability and cutting-edge technology
- **Professional Appeal**: Suitable for business and career contexts

## 📊 Accessibility Analysis

### Contrast Ratios (WCAG Compliance)
- **Black on White**: 21:1 (Excellent)
- **Gray-900 on Gray-50**: ~15:1 (Excellent)
- **Blue-600 on White**: ~4.5:1 (Good)
- **Purple-600 on White**: ~4.2:1 (Good)

### Color Blindness Considerations
- **Blue-Purple**: Generally safe for most color vision deficiencies
- **Avoid**: Red-green combinations
- **Test**: Use tools like Color Oracle for validation

## 🎨 Color Usage Guidelines

- **Main CTA Buttons**: Blue-to-purple gradient

- **Focus States**: Blue with ring effect

### 2. Secondary Actions
- **Secondary Buttons**: White with blue border
- **Ghost Buttons**: Blue text on transparent background
- **Link States**: Blue with purple hover

### 3. Content Hierarchy
- **Headings**: Dark gray (#111827)
- **Body Text**: Medium gray (#6b7280)

### 4. Status Indicators
- **Success**: Green (#10b981)
- **Warning**: Orange (#f59e0b)
